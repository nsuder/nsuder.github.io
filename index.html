Ottl

A local-first memory for your workday, for macOS.

Ottl turns what you say into text: dictation into any app, notes to yourself, and transcripts of the calls you choose to record. From those it keeps a private memory of the people, projects, decisions and commitments in your work, stored on your own computer.

If you connect Google Calendar, Ottl reads the event you are in when a call starts, so the recording gets the meeting's title and the right people's names.

Ottl is in early testing and is not yet publicly available.
