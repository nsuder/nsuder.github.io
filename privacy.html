Privacy policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

The short version

Ottl keeps what it learns on your computer. It has no account system, no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising, and it never sells data.

What Ottl stores, and where

Transcripts, notes, and the memory built from them (people, companies, projects, decisions, commitments) are stored in a database on your Mac. Call audio is kept only until it has been transcribed, then deleted. You can delete any conversation, or all of Ottl's data, from your computer at any time.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Ottl asks for read-only access to your calendar events (calendar.events.readonly). When a call starts, Ottl reads the events around that time to find the one you are in, and saves its title, start and end time, meeting link, and the names, email addresses and responses of its invitees with that conversation, on your computer. Ottl never creates, changes or deletes calendar events.

The sign-in token is stored in your Mac's Keychain. You can disconnect in Ottl, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Ottl's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide Ottl's features to you; it is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI models, and not read by people except with your permission or as required by law.

Services Ottl uses

To transcribe speech and to understand what was said, Ottl sends audio and text to an AI service you set up with your own key (currently Groq). When identifying who spoke on a call, the request can include the names of the event's invitees. These requests go directly from your computer to that service under its own terms; Ottl does not keep a copy anywhere else. Ottl does not send your Google data to any other party.
